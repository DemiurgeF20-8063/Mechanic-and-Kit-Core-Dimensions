
# Fustix Tufted.

***Homeworld***: Nahrulos. Eidolian Galaxy. Dimension 1A1-66-115
***Spreading***: Eidolian Galaxy, Dimension 1A1-66-115 (Before second Great War). Gasia, Gahvarian galaxy, unknown dimension (After second Great War)
***Comments***: Species, to which belongs Diana Clum (CaR, HoS, FaN) and Alarein Suunt (EoS) as well as non-player characters such as Talia Solis, Sofis Axiom. The species' name derives from the word "fuse," which in this case means to merge or fuse. The species is a fusion of plants and animals, as they are furred, warm-blooded, feed on milk, and are viviparous, but obtain almost all their nutrients through photosynthesis. In the Russian localization, the word "Фустик" is similar to "Лунтик" (”Luntik”). The name is partly due to the four ears on their heads. The name of their homeworld is derived from the word "Nahrungslos," meaning "foodless”.

# Fustix Fluffy.

***Homeworld***: Nahrulos. Eidolian Galaxy. Dimension 2A1-66-115
***Spreading***: Wandered from dimension to dimension. (Before second Great War). Gasia, Gahvarian galaxy, unknown dimension (After second Great War).
***Comments***: The species to which  belongs the heroes, Jord Solis (RoM), Apocrys Axiom (MaD), as well as non-player characters such as Selena Kriptos, Kipris Axion, and Letos Suunt. Since the dimensions 1A1-66-115 are exact copies of each other, two identical species emerged within them. However, due to the imperfect duplication of events in 2A1-66-115, a cataclysm occurred on Nahrulos, causing the planet to become cold. Their biology, attempting to adapt to the new conditions, developed fluffy tails, stripes on head and added bangs to the foreheads of females, forming the Fustix Fluffy race. Fustix Tufted and Fustix Fluffy are fully compatible with each other, producing healthy and fertile offspring.

# Grable

***Homeworld***: Refussi. Tonata Galaxy
***Spreading***: Refussi, Walthm
***Comments***: The word is derived from the word "grab." In the Russian localization, it's derived from two words: “игра” (game) and “лоб” (forehead), meaning "playing foreheads." This is because they constantly spend time looking at screen-based devices. They are short, thin, and naturally hunched due to centuries of sedentary technological life. They possess an elongated head with a massively thickened, smooth forehead bone—an evolutionary biological shield developed to protect the brain from the electromagnetic radiation of constant display usage. Their eyes are large but deeply set under heavy brow ridges, highly sensitive to the blue light spectrum of monitors but squinting heavily in natural sunlight. Their skin is pale, ash-green, and completely devoid of heavy pigmentation due to life in artificial industrial environments. They have three elongated, highly agile fingers on each hand, perfectly adapted for rapid coding and interface manipulation.
This is the species to which **Chairman Quand** belongs—the secondary antagonist of _Ask From Heroism_ (AfH), who commands industrial empires and warbot armies with a mere tap of his three fingers, while the ultimate ideological battle of the game is reserved for the clash between Witya and the false icon, **Unlas**.

# Ceroscanian

***Homeworld***: Ceroscan. Tonata Galaxy
***Spreading***: One representative
***Comments***: The species became extinct 57 years before the events of Ask from Heroism as a result of a pandemic. Only one member, Linuscus Leslie Unlas, survived after being separated from an excursion and lost on a jungle planet. The species is a large, humanoid species, nearly 3 meters tall, with abundant, strong, and defined muscles, and three fingers on each hand. They are hermaphrodites and often suffer from narcissism. After the pandemic, their home planet was inhabited by the Timzers.

# Jagurs

***Homeworld***: Jaguria. Tonata Galaxy.
***Spreading***: Tonata Galaxy.
***Comments***: The game features one representative of this race, Sara Benolux. However, the species is not extinct and travels the galaxy, though its population is not as large. They can be encountered on Ecupoli III as NPCs—ordinary workers at Gismotron. The word "Jagur" comes from the word "jaguar," although in the Russian localization it sounds more like the word "cucumber," although they are unrelated. They are extremely human-like, with five fingers on their hands, hairless skin, and no hair, but they have hair on their heads similar to that of humans. Their faces are also similar to those of humans. They have elf-like, pointed ears. They lack a tail at birth, but one grows by puberty and takes the form of a leather whip with a wider end on the right and left sides. Their skin color varies and they have pigment spots reminiscent of a jaguar.

# Meltinian

***Homeworld***: Meltin. Tonata Galaxy
***Spreading***: Meltin.
***Comments***: The species to which Boris Hartocat and Fedor Nakagawin belong. They have an extremely small and isolated population, numbering only tens of thousands due to the harsh, unforgiving ecosystem of their homeworld. Meltin is a hyper-arid, rocky, and static desert planet. Due to its small planetary mass, its core has long cooled down, leaving the planet as a solid stone with completely absent tectonic or volcanic activity—its surface is shaped entirely by extreme atmospheric temperature drops and violent abrasive sandstorms. To survive this environment, Meltinians evolved as short, stocky **sandy-brown** humanoids with a high bone density. Their dust-colored skin serves as a natural evolutionary camouflage, allowing them to blend seamlessly with the sandstone cliffs and craggy environments of their static homeworld. Their hands feature three thick, powerful fingers with rough, calloused tips adapted for climbing jagged cliffs and operating heavy scrap machinery. Their most distinctive evolutionary trait is the presence of numerous rough, heavy bony growths (osteoderms) forming natural skeletal plates across their faces. These growths act as a mandatory biological shield, protecting their facial tissues, eyes, and respiratory pathways from high-velocity rock fragments and micro-particles during dust storms.

# Lacrets

***Homeworld***: Watlone. Tonata Galaxy
***Spreading***: Tonata Galaxy
***Comments***: The species to which Gerald Pastrunas, Ross Pastrunas, Reism Headroof, and Sellof Sterces belong. The word is derived from Lacerta, the Latin word for lizard. An anthropomorphic reptilian species characterized by a short, vestigial tail, and three agile fingers on each hand. Their entire bodies are covered in small, dense, vibrant blue scales. Their home planet, Watlone, is a tropical ocean world operating on a highly accelerated orbital period. Due to the planet's rapid transit around its star, the calendar year is compressed, creating extreme and volatile seasonal shifts: half of the brief year is dominated by scorching, intense tropical heat and blinding sunlight, while the other half immediately plunges into a freezing period of relentless torrential rains and global monsoons. Their blue scales evolved as a mandatory dual-purpose biological shield: during the hot season, the reflective, tightly locked structure of the scales mitigates intense infrared solar heat, preventing rapid dehydration and skin cracking under the tropical sun. During the hyper-humid wet season, the hydrophobic scales offer perfect thermal protection, shedding water instantly and protecting the Lacrets against hypothermia in Watlone's vast, turbulent oceans.

# Ramsunian

***Homeworld***: Leakwildia. Tonata Galaxy.
***Spreading***: Leakwildia.
***Comments***: The word derives from "ramson," meaning wild garlic. In Russian, wild onions are also called "Черемша.” The game features one representative of this race, Sveta Bennux. However, the species is not extinct; they simply rarely leave their homeworld, the administrative capital of the Tonata Galaxy. They have five fingers and are covered in fur ranging from dark yellow to dark brown. Their faces are cat-like, but their mouths are straight, like those of humans. Their ears are rabbit-like. Males have a mane on their necks, the back and up of their heads, while females' hair is no longer than their main fur and lacks hair similar to that of humans. Both males and females have a tufted tail.

# Timzers

***Homeworld***: Unknown
***Spreading***: Tonata Galaxy, Eidolian Galaxy
***Comments***: The word comes from the English word "time." Yes, the very same Timzers who destroyed the first iteration of the Living World as a result of chaotic time travel have re-formed, but have acquired a scar in the form of a rare stutter. They are short creatures with a plump build, maroon skin, and a large number of wrinkles. Their heads have two horn-like appendages curving toward their chins. The species' home planet is unknown, as the Timzers, like the first iteration, were among the first intelligent species to enter the space age. They have now spread across many planets, including infesting Ceroscan after the pandemic. Despite their antiquity, the species is not distinguished by its technological superiority over others.
