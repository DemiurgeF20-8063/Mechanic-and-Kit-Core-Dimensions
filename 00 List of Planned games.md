# List of planned games
[[MULTIVERSE DIMENSION UTILIZATION LICENSE (M&K WORLD LICENSE)]]

This article contains a list of planned games and a brief plot summary. A total of 23 are planned: 12 main games (a triplet of quadrologies) and 11 spin-offs with canonical events. Some might say that's a lot. I'll say yes, I know, I understand perfectly well. But this will keep me busy for a while. And maybe someone else will join me later. I didn't prepare this much space for myself alone. Yes, revealing the plots of planned games might not be the best decision, because if a miracle happens, and it comes down to it, a lot of people will know what the games will be about... But miracles have never happened to me before. This list is needed to give the reader a correct timeline so that he does not get confused about which campaign is which, and also, due to the fact that I often use abbreviations instead of the full name, so that anyone can look at this list and see which game this abbreviation refers to.
Well...

# 01 Ask from Heroism (AfH)

***Plot:*** Kit arrives in the Living World to learn empathy and meets with the Mechanic, giving him the final component to complete his homemade spaceship. Over the course of their shared adventures, they give each other the names Witya and Katya. Thanks to the spaceship, Witya manages to leave Meltin and join the ranks of Tonata's Rangers, as he had long dreamed. Only later does it become clear that the Rangers' leader, Linuscus Leclie Unlas, has sold out to Quand Industries and was never a hero at all, merely projecting one. As a result, Witya is forced to confront the Quand's Warbots alone for most of the time, and ultimately ends up engaging Unlas himself.

# 01-02 Cloning of Lonelyness (CoL) (Spinoff)

***Plot:*** (roughly): Witya is enjoying his vacation at the Del-Alfomar Bohemi Resort. A girl approaches him and asks to pose for a photo shoot. He agrees, but suddenly, agressive combat robots arrive in place of the small crabs, and at the end girl flies off with them. Trying to figure out what's that was, he visits several planets, after which a mysterious stranger, the leader of the Nanomite faction, contacts him, promising to tell him about his family. However, this turns out to be a trap: his genetic material is taken and clones are created, which become an army for the mysterious stranger. However, these clones turn out to be far less combat-ready, which is why Witya manages to defeat all of them and the mysterious stranger himself.

# 02 Cannot agree Reject (CaR)

***Plot:*** Witya's friend, Boris Hartocat, turns out to be a criminal and, with the support of the Nanomites, escapes from prison. Witya pursues him, which leads him to Ellesmere galaxy, beyond the jurisdiction of the Tonata Rangers. After a shootout on the city streets, both are duly imprisoned, after which they are hired by Pantocorp to recover a Pantopet stolen by a mysterious thief. After an initial fight with the thief, Boris escapes. Thief revealing to be Diana Clum, a Fustix tufted who lost her tail. She tells Witya why she started all this, and Witya ultimately sides with her. The two of them, fighting side by side, oppose Pentocorp and its CEO, Pieterszoo Coen, or rather, whoever happens to be in his place.

***Plot:*** Despite all the adventures, one has to live on something, and Witya agrees to play the role of a criminal in a show in which Katya was the main character. At the end, in the real world, Witya learns that Meltin has been attacked, and together with Diana, they set off to defend their home.

# 03 Hopes of Soulless (HoS)

***Plot:*** The Tonata government declares war on the Nanomites due to the incident with Boris Hartocat, and at that moment, the Terrelipus race strikes them from behind. Witya and Diana arrive on Meltin, but they prove insufficient to stop the advance of the Terrelipuses. Meltin is ultimately blown up. Afterwards, Diana and Witya set off to rescue Unlas, who has gone mad after a prison riot and erased his memory. As events unfold, it becomes clear that Doctor Sience is behind it all. He was turned into a robot by Unlas's inadvertent push into the converter, and he wants to turn everyone into robots because of having been placed in the "tool" caste after the incident, as all robots.

# 03-04 Flesh is Formattable (FiF) (Spinoff)

***Plot:*** While reverse-engineering a matter-conversion device, Witya and Katya accidentally trigger it, causing them to be digitized and tethered to the internet. They manage to contact Gerald Pastrunas, but he's denied a day off, resulting in the device for re-converting information back into flesh being sent only in fragments to different parts of the internet. However, Katya's main enemy from the show Secret Agent Kit also learns of their plans and intends to become material himself.

# 04 Death on Company (DoC)

***Plot:*** Tonata's Rangers are constructing a military training station and a large combat robot factory. However, the project's manager, Claimore Bood, tricks the entire staff into wearing explosive collars and blackmails into blowing them up if the Tonata government interferes. At the station, which he calls Cooloseum, he stages gladiatorial combat between heroes, abducting them from different dimensions using a Melodionator that has found its way onto the black market. Witya, sedated by a sleeping gas, and Dr. Sience are caught in this arena. Witya and Sience agree to help each other escape the station, one by creating a spare head and the other by recreating a matter conversion device. The move with Sience's head clockworks, but the device doesn't. Witya ends up cloned into the network instead of being digitized. As a result, the real Witya remains in the arena and is forced to continue fighting, including against the current champion, Semyon Dedlight. And one of the raids to kidnap another hero even leads to a war between organics and robots in dimension 1H7-47-298.

# 04-05 Cloning into Freedom (CiF) (Spin-off)

***Plot:*** Witya's Clone quickly finds a device for reconverting information back into flesh, but the network has been flooded with Claimore Bood viruses, making it difficult to regain access to the device. As soon as Witya's Clone manages to become corporeal, Tonata's Rangers are discovered storming the station. Doctor Sience has figured out how the system works and how to jam it, and has made a deal with the Tonata government. Witya joins the assault, breaks into the main arena, and engages in battle with Claimore Bood. He meets the original Vitya and flies to the Eidolon galaxy at the invitation of the Platifishes, who have come to retrieve Bood's Melodyonator.

# 05 Nothingness of Ancestors (NoA)

***Plot:*** The Clone arrives in Eidolian, but quickly suspects something is wrong and storms the ship, then parks at Hepunkt Station, where he meets Dina Hepunkt. However, Witya and Diana Clum are hot on his heels. Diana decides to remain in the shadows for now. The Clone and Witya, fighting together, discover that the Fustix were exterminated by Lumenor Antechent. According to Antechent himself, every single one. With the tacit consent of the Eidolian Defense Forces (EDF), who feared his Platifish army. Ultimately, Witya and the Clone are forced to fight the entire army alone. Along the way, they discover a broken ISORI, which Witya believes is a broken Fustix's computer. Katya and Chords repair the device, and the ISORI returns to its rightful post. During the first battle with Antechent himself, he opens a portal supposedly to the Fustix dimension, where the Clone is sent. Diana jumps to him, and promises to bring him back. Witya spends the rest of the campaign alongside Dina Hepunkt. At the end, Witya refuses to kill Antechent, instead using the Symphony Shard to send him to dimension 2A1-66-115.

ISORI agreed.
Witya arrives at the ---as yet unnamed--- combat complex and participates in arena battles. His final opponent is Alarein Suunt, another Fustix. They engage in a friendly fight, after which they escape the arena together and set off in search of Great Breguet. Katya dislikes this idea and, knowing the history of ISORI, tells Witya that it will break Great Breguet. Shortly before the events, Dr. Sience arrives in Eidolian and, learning of the heroes' plans, opposes them. Sience promises the local robots rights and freedoms, but in reality, he only uses them against the protagonists, fearing that they will undo his birth. He begins searching for a Great Breguet himself to undo his own robotization. However, as everyone searches, the ISORI realizes that a victorious Shrampit empire from 2A1-66-115 will become an unstoppable threat to the entire Living World and gives Katya a hint about the seven minutes. In the final scene, Alarein kills Witya, and Katya understands why she was given this hint. After the rollback, Witya dodges Alarein's shot due to phantom pain, which brings him to his knees. He doesn't remember his death, but his body does. Clone and Diana arrive, but Witya persuades them not to attack Alarein. Alarein begins his rollback. Breguet would have held out. That was his plan. Only Kate convinced Witya that this would break Breguet. And Clone and Diana, who had arrived to see Witya, are on his side. Alarein is one against three. And due to the slight instability, everyone concludes that Katya is right. Alarein has no choice but to stop Breguet, sacrificing himself. The Great Breguet is no longer functional. All power over reality passes back to the ISORI.

# 07 From Ancient Nether (FaN)

***Plot:*** Two unknown criminals break into the Fustix Historical Museum and steal the Melodyonator in an attempt to open a rift to their home dimension, 2X9-36-918, a Class X dimension, meaning it is infested with Nether. Only this Melodyonator turns out to be damaged, which is why they not only open a portal to this dimension, but chaotic rifting begins and creatures from this dimension begin to break through. The initial stage of Nether infection begins, which is opposed by the Eidolian defense forces and, alternately, the Clone, then Witya himself, with the support of Diana. During the confrontation, Diana's Melodyonator is destroyed. At the end, one of the Nether Leaders appears and kills one of the criminals. The second goes over to the side of the heroes, after which he voluntarily surrenders to the EDF. And Witya refuses to continue searching for the Fustixes, since he cannot leave this dimension unattended and traveling between dimensions can be very dangerous.

# 08 Shadows of One (SoO)

***Plot:*** Katya restores the Melodionator without Witya's knowledge, while Dr. Sience figures out the dimension in which he always wins. When it's time to give Witya his present, the gift is given to the Clone. At that moment, Dr. Sience attacks the parade. Witya misses the fight because he's listening to music on his headphones, and Diana is busy preparing food for the Pantopets. Eventually, Partys jumps out of the first rift into the dimension where Sience always wins and is shocked by what's happening around her. The Clone is also shocked, as he never expected to encounter the Fustix in such a way. They both eventually cross over to dimension 1H7-47-298 and battle the Doctor, but Partys damages the Melodyonator, trapping The Clone in dimension 1H7-47-298. Clone and Partys, traveling and fighting together, are forced to build a new one, while simultaneously growing closer to each other. But Katya advises holding off on romance, as, in her opinion, Partys could only be a spatial copy of Witya. A Clone with an identity crisis. And next to him is Partys. Two shadows of a single personality. He looks at Partys and sees that Partys is independent. She had her own personal, fulfilling life, completely unrelated to Witya's. Who, if not Partys, can convince the Clone that being a copy of someone doesn't negate individuality and independence? And Partys herself will have an example of someone who truly is a "copy." Meanwhile, Witya has caused trouble in dimension 1H7-47-298, participating in the Grable rebellion, and the Clone will have to deal with the consequences of the original. Along the way, they discover abandoned archives on a planet ---as yet unnamed--- containing a complete dimensional map with a marker for the Fustix dimension. Eventually, the Melodyonator falls into the hands of Emperor Sience, the Doctor's dimensional counterpart, who opens a portal to Witya's home dimension to exact revenge on Claimore Bood for starting a war in his dimension. It is in Witya's home dimension that Emperor Sience and Doctor Sience are defeated, and the Clone, along with Partis, decide to finally venture into the Fustix dimension.

The only idea that comes to mind is to open an interdimensional rift directly from within this field. As soon as they obtain this information, these forces attack the Chords' dimension. They head there to protect the creators, but the forces are clearly uneven. Selena tells the Chords to contact the Fustixes, that they will come to their aid. # 12 Goal of Doom (GoD)

***Plot:*** The combined forces of dimension 1A1-66-115 fight again. This time, it's the power of all 35 million Nether dimensions, which have torn through reality in order to improve it, to perfect it. Naturally, 1A1-66-115 doesn't stand a chance. Afterwards, the Clone, having learned about the existence of 14^7 worlds in the Siren's arena, conceives the idea that the beings living there can tell Nether where he could have acted better. They help three Fustix from the game of Unlas materialize, and they tell Nether alternatives. Nether, realizing his actions were imperfect, begins to destroy himself. But one of the Architects survives, and Nether makes his final stand. But as soon as Partys arrives (canonically, she was infected) and destroys the last Architect... At that moment, the Architect she was infected with flies out of her. During her ultimate attack on Chord, this Architect didn't "burn out," but rather became so overwhelmed by her naivety and uncompromising nature that he was able to repel and destroy the Nether around him. They open fire on him, but he easily defends himself and declares that he is no longer their enemy. That they have destroyed all threats, eradicated all evil, and now there is nothing left for them but peaceful life. That the Nether has achieved its goal. The Living World is "perfect."
