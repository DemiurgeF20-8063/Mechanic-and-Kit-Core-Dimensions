## Planets&Locations
### Meltin (AfH, HoS)

The name comes from the word Melt. An inconspicuous planet, home to the underprivileged. One-year-old newborn Witya was abandoned on Kownool Plateau. As Crutch's adviced. His father, Scad Peros, missed Encapsulation and tried to hide Witya from Antichent. At the end of the AfH campaign, the planet was on the brink of destruction. Quand wanted to blow it up with an Acatadop (Autonomous Collector After The Absolute Destruction Of Planet) and place the Wealth Refussi in its orbit, but Witya and Tonata's Rangers prevented him. Following the events of the Hopes of Soulless campaign, the planet was attacked by the ---No Name Yet--- race. Tonata's regular army was unable to withstand the onslaught, resulting in the planet being blown up along with the enemy forces, marking a turning point.

The planet contains two locations:

###### Kowloon Plateau.
The name comes from the Kowloon Walled City. This location is home to Witya's garage and the small settlement where Boris Hartocat is holed up.

###### Acatadop (2nd visit).
The planet was to be destroyed to free up orbit for Wealth Refussi, a self-built planet of Quand Industries. Here, not Tonata's Rangers, but Witya himself engages the remnants of the warbots and Grable mercenaries with the goal of gutting Acatadop, preserving his home, despite Witya's dream of escaping it, and bringing Chairman Quand to justice. More precisely, to lynching...

### Ceroscan (AfH)

The planet's name comes from a combination of the words "hero" and "scan." It's also somewhat similar to Coruscant. The home planet of the Ceroscanians, who became extinct 57 years before the events of AfH as a result of a pandemic. It is an ecumenopolis, the cultural capital of the Tonata galaxy.

The planet contains four locations.

###### Pretory City.
The name comes from Pretoria, the administrative capital of South Africa. Despite the planet being an ecumenopolis, the city itself has a separate name.

###### Pretory Sky.
The sky above the city where Witya and his homemade aircraft, which turned out to be quite capable, engage in aerial combat with the Quand Industries fleet.

###### Hall of Rangers.
The headquarters of the Tonata Rangers before this "superhero" team disbanded and the brand was purchased by the newly formed Tonata regular army. This headquarters hosted all sorts of events, from briefings to interviews, from candidate acceptance to combat training.

###### Rangers Games.
A series of rigorous trials designed to select the most worthy candidates for the Tonata Rangers. A beautiful show also showcases the rigorous selection process for the cadets. In reality, these are extremely corrupt games, where the tests are played out in a giveaway manner with pre-arranged candidates who have political, financial or family authority.

### Cooloseum Station (DoC, CiF)

This name comes from a combination of two words: cool and lose. It also sounds similar to the word Colosseum.The station was originally built as a military training ground and a robot factory for the Tonata Rangers. However, Claimore Bood tricked everyone into wearing explosive collars, taking a large number of creatures hostage and then blackmailing the Tonata government.

Using the Melodyonator, which had found its way onto the black market due to the events with Darla Suunt, he gathered heroes from various dimensions and pitted them against each other in a gladiatorial arena for show. However, a loophole was discovered in the Bood's system, leading to a decision to storm the station. In addition to Tonata's regular Army, Clone of Witya and Doctor Sience participated in the assault. Following the assault, according to the agreement, the station passed into Doctor's hands and was henceforth known as Sience Station.

This station contains the following locations:

###### Rest Blocks.
This is the location of the main rest area for gladiators between trials. It also serves as a spaceport for departures to trials held outside the station.

###### Main Arena.
This is where the actual gladiator fights take place. It is also where the final battle between Claimore Bood and Witya took place.

###### Transformable Runway.
This is an arena where the gladiator's main test is not combat, but stunt skills.

###### Maintenance Rooms.
These are unoccupied areas where Clone Vitya and Doctor Science fought their way to the main arena with the support of Tonata's Rangers during the final assault.

### Mammonia (AfH)

A cold planet with ammonia oceans and aggressive fauna. It is there that Witya arrives in search of a solution of uranium tetraammine in liquid ammonia—UF4(NH3)4—for D.Y.N.O. Since this world is radiation-free and the atomic nucleus is completely stable, this emerald liquid can be used as an active medium for high-power tunable liquid lasers or as an optical filter of variable density, absorbing strictly defined wavelengths.
The planet contains two locations:

###### Maburgtum Research Complex.
The name comes from the fusion of the words "Technical University of Munich (TUM)" and "Philipps University of Marburg." These two universities were the ones that discovered Uranium Tetraammine. It is in this complex that Vitya discovers this component. Remarkably, despite Witya landing some distance from the complex, the area around it doesn't have a specific name.

###### Maburgtum Orbit.
This is where the space battle between Witya, in the official, licensed combat spacecraft of the Tonata Rangers, and a group of space pirates takes place.

### Refussi (Before AfH)

The name of this planet comes from the word "refuse"—a term used in environmental standards to describe dense, often mixed industrial waste that is "rejected" by production due to toxicity or the impossibility of recycling.  
This world is native to the Grable race. However, their compatriot, Quand, created Quand Industries and polluted the planet with industrial waste to the point of being uninhabitable. He then decided to create another planet and arrange for the resettlement of the Grable for a certain price.

The planet contains one location:

###### Acatadop (1st visit).
It was here that the Acatadop was parked for maintenance during the first coordinated raid by the Tonata Rangers.

### Walthm (AfH)

The name comes from Waltham, Massachusetts, the home of the terrifying leaping robots. This planet is home to a subsidiary of Quand Industries, which assembles combat robots. The most current series is nicknamed "Warbot." These robots form the backbone of Quand's army, but he also employ Grable mercenaries for certain operations.

Planet contains one location:

###### Costmesa Crater
The name comes from Costa Mesa, the CIA's Silicon Valley. As a mock battle, Tonata's Rangers attack the factory with the aim of destroying it. However, the simulated battle goes awry, and the lead stormtrooper, Brats Weales, suffers a wound that renders him incapable of combat. This precipitates the collapse of the organization.

### Gotver (AfH)

The name comes from the word Gatver, which translates from Dutch as "This Place - Hole." Another word, "Gotver," is pronounced similar to "Got there." On this planet, Gerald Pastrunas, on the advice of Reism Headroof, wanted to open a hoverskate racing track.

Planet contains two location:

###### Inculand.
Word comes from Italian word "Inculandia" - "This Land - Ass". Here, Vitya stumbles upon Reism Headroof's escape pod and, at his request, searches for the pod with Gerald Pastrunas, fighting off Soilshark.

###### Inculand factory,
From which Witya, through a raider takeover, obtains parts to repair Gerald's ship, which had a rather rough landing. Who owns this factory and what it produces is unknown.

I honestly thought there was a mission with it in the first campaign, but when I started rereading the enemy and weapon lists, I realized this planet isn't in the first campaign and... The story written here doesn't exist. Not in this campaign or any other. Now I don't know what to do with it. It'll hang here for now, and then I'll figure out how to rewrite it to fit the events of another campaign.

The name comes from a combination of the words "Mini" and "Kadia." A sovereign, independent, and highly militarized planet. During the AfH campaign, it was attacked by Quand Industries forces with the covert support of Tonata's Rangers, as the government viewed civilizations with a militaristic cult as a threat, even though the civilization inhabiting it was territorial and did not display aggression without provocation. Despite its isolationist policy, the planet appealed to Tonata's Rangers for assistance, and Witya responded. The attack on the planet was repelled.

Planet contains one location:

###### Minkagia MDN
MDN is main defensive node. It is in the battles for this node that Witya joins in, preventing the destruction of WASD - PC (Weapon of Anti-Space Defense - Plasma Cannon), and Katya, controlling WASD - PC manually, clears out Grable corvettes.

### Watlone (AfH)
The name of this planet comes from a combination of the words "water" and "lone." This planet is covered by vast oceans and has a tropical climate, meaning half the year is hot and sunny, and half is cold and rainy, although the year is not very long due to the planet's rapid orbit.

Planet contains two location:

###### City of Oden
Name comes from the Yemeni port of Aden, one of the most notorious smuggling hubs. And, yes... Witya arrives on this planet in search of a smuggler who can provide him with one of the components for assembling the D.Y.N.O. This smuggler turns out to be Sellof Sterces. However, the city is on lockdown due to a Slimenoid infestation, making the meeting not without a fight.

###### Port of Oden
Just an additional location through which Witya walked in search of the Unbinil Bolt

### Harmon Quit (Before AfH)

A planet that is the source of Resonant Charoite in this galaxy. It was blown up by Quand on the advice of Diana Klum, who was attempting to destroy all the paired Charoites in dimension 1A1-66-115 in order to sever the connection with the paired dimension and prevent the Shrimpit Empire from infiltrating this world.

### Rotham Nebula (AfH)

The name comes from Rothamsted Research, Hertfordshire, UK, the world's oldest agrobiological research institute, founded in 1843.In the AfH campaign.

Nebula contains one location:

###### GSDM station
GSDM is Grable's Systematic destructors manufacturer. Witya arrives here on a top-secret mission from Linuscus Leslie Unlas, which turns out to be his trap.This station was engaged in genetic experiments and the breeding of synthetic species for use in combat. Due to the relative similarity of their activities (genetics, breeding), the decision was made to give this nebula this name.

### Charra (AfH)

The name comes from Char, a scorched lava hell covered in rivers of magma that became the main hive for the Zerg race. And, yes... This planet is a lava planet. And it's even home to Zerg-like creatures. But more on that in the locations.

Planet contains two location:

###### GRO
Grable Research Outpost. This outpost was actively involved in supporting and developing new versions of warbots for Quand Industries, as well as equipment for Grable mercenaries. Witya arrived to clear the outpost on a mission given to him by Sara Benolux, not Unlas.

###### Brainrot Lava.
The lava fields adjacent to the outpost. Not only are Systematic Destroyers, an invasive species from GSDM, located here, but also the indigenous inhabitants of these fields: Brain-eaters. A swarm of creatures that, for some unknown reason, by eating the brains of other creatures, increase their own brain size without becoming any smarter. It is these brains that one of Grable's scientists asks Witya to obtain in exchange for several gadgets he will need for his future adventures.

### Ecupoli III (AfH)

Another planet that's an ecumenopolis. Considering that most planets are only 200-500 km in radius, building an entire planet isn't such an impossible task. The planet is the headquarters of Gismotron, a megacorporation that manufactures consumer electronics of all types.

The planet contains three locations:

###### FYA Center
FYA stands for Youth Attraction. A small office where Witya can sign up for hoverskate racing and also purchase some household appliances that can be used as weapons, like the Barbeque Maker—a portable device for cooking meat, but actually a flamethrower—or the Seeder—a device for spraying seedlings, the cassettes of which can easily be filled with explosives.

###### Gismotron Skating Track
It's here, of course, that hoverskate races are held with various participants from across the galaxy, and Vitya can participate. For a reward, of course. However, injury prevention was the last thing on the racing track's organizers' minds.

###### Repair Shop.
A typical repair and maintenance department for the corporation's products. However, for Witya, it became a special place due to his friendship with Gerald Pastrunas, formed after saving him. Racing is all well and good, but he wasn't a very successful racer, unlike his father, Valentino Pastrunas, so he had to work at a much less extreme job. Gerald was very good at tinkering with technology, but it wasn't much fun for him. It's here that Witya can install legal and not-so-legal upgrades on Katya, and Gerald also helps Witya assemble the D.Y.N.O.

### Opozitum (AfH)

The name comes from the word "opposition." This planet is known for its distinctive political views and opinions on many galactic issues. The planet has representatives in several opposition parties in the Galactic Congress, but when the parties begin to argue, it's usually the representatives from this planet who do the arguing. Unlike most ultra-futuristic cities, the cities on this planet are more reminiscent of 18th- to early 19th-century towns in architecture, although most of their streets are quite wide and intertwined with trees. There are some ultra-futuristic elements that break up the organic picture.

The planet contains one location:

###### Opi-nioh city
The name comes from the word "opinion." It is here that Vitya engages in battle with the Quand Industries warbots who attempted to assassinate Senator Chazev Nimsov.

### Del-Alfomar (AfH, SiD)

The name comes from the artificial, isolated resort of San Alfonso del Mar, Chile. It's an island-type resort planet. It experiences no seasonal fluctuations thanks to its circular orbit and the lack of axial tilt. These factors made it an ideal resort planet. No intelligent life developed there, so the first colonists arrived on the uninhabited planet and decided to turn it into a free-market zone. The planet is divided into plots that are sold or leased to resort operators.
The planet contains two locations:

###### Bohemi Skies
Here, an aerial battle ensues between Witya and the Watersporters. These are special, segmented tankers developed by Quand Industries to deliver water to his makeshift planet.

###### Bohemi Resort
The name comes from Bohemian Grove, California—a secret vacation spot for governments around the world. This is what Fedor Nakagawin called his piece of the planet. He had become rich under unknown circumstances, and, remarkably, also hails from Meltin. After the battle with the Watersporters, Fedor asks Witya to redistribute the oceans using a special pipe system and a device he calls the Hydroswaper, as the water stealers were draining the oceans unevenly.

### Del-Alfomar (Dimension 2G4-17-055) (RoM)

The Quand Industries equivalent in dimension 2G4-17-055, instead of dumping industrial waste on its own planet, began dumping it on the resort planet Del-Alfomar. It's unknown how it obtained permission to do this, or... whether it was necessary. As a result of the pollution, the local flora and fauna mutated, giving rise to an incredibly aggressive but useful species called the Fishbeast. Their meat is quite tasty, their bones are made into soap, their nerves are made into rope, and even their eyeballs and lymph have found uses. The flora also proves useful. Species with primitive neurobiology, as Slimenoid, can inhale the smoke from the leaves of an unspecified plant and derive pleasure from it.
The planet contains two locations:

###### Open Ocean.
Due to inaccurate coordinates, the rift opened not near the Fishhunter shack, forcing the heroes—Jord Solis, Partys Solis, and Witya Peros—to fight their way through Fishbeast-infested oceans between islands for part of their route.

###### The Fishhunter Shack.
Not a shack, but a fully-fledged, fortified outpost that would be the envy of many Mincadia defense hubs. This shack is home to the Fishhunters—a squad of hunters led by Muon, a Unlas-like figure. They regularly launch raids, delivering fresh Fishbeast carcasses for butchering, which are then processed at the base. Jord Solis himself served in this squad for a time when he needed to earn extra bolts. It was during one of these raids that he lost his tail. This base was also responsible for the collection and distribution of Quand Industries' waste.
The heroes had to break into this shack, since it was the closest route to the Siren's Socket station, since the station itself was moving in orbit and its coordinates were extremely difficult to determine accurately.

## Siren's Socket Station (Dimension 2G4-17-055)

A station orbiting Del Alfomar at a fairly high speed. The heroes arrive at this station to meet Siren, the station's owner, as the name suggests, to purchase Resonance Plates. The first plate is obtained through the Quest, and another can be found in the center of the station, as well as through arena battles. However, one still has to be purchased for 200,000 bolts.
The station contains five locations:
###### Club Zone
The station's main entertainment complex. It houses a large number of bars and clubs. This place is extremely popular due to the availability of an extremely rare commodity in the entire Living World: syrup. Or, in ΛCDM parlance, alcohol. Who knows, maybe polluted Del Alfomar has contributed to this. In one of these bars, you can meet Selena Kriptos, playing a game of knife-and-knives for bottles of syrup. She will avoid interaction with Witya and Jord, recognizing them by their scent, but you can interact with her when playing as Partys. There's a sauna here where Jord can wash himself, as the soap there is made from Fishbeast bones. Unlike in most other worlds, soap is made from Charoite Powder, to which Jord is allergic. You can meet the Siren herself in this zone after completing her quest. In Challenge mode, you can find PDG - 294 (Perfect Drink Generator - 294) here. It has its own ARG gameplay thread.

###### Arena
A combat complex created by the Siren to hone the combat AI's behavior. If the heroes agree to participate, Siren will secretly scan their brains and bring them suitable opponents into the arena. However, during the final trials, she will delve too deeply, first uncovering Witya's personal problem and then completely breaking through the 14^7 barrier, bringing into this world several personalities who could have been born in place of our heroes.

###### A socket for everyone.
A small settlement for the station's maintenance personnel, as well as a hotel. It's bustling with life, and since the staff works several shifts, it's crowded at all times. There are markets, local vendors, and businesses. Work on the station is in demand because Fishbeast meat is affordable even to ordinary workers, while in the rest of the galactic market it's considered a delicacy. It's also a major smuggling hub, protected by Siren herself. Well, well... actively involved, the boss of all smuggling.

###### Abandoned Zone.
A huge, empty residential area where Slinenoids operated before Siren asked the protagonists to clear it out. What this area was for, what it was, or who lived there before is completely unknown. Rumor has it that Fishhunters used to be based here, or that this area was specifically built to evacuate the Shack's personnel in case of an emergency. Siren doesn't divulge the purpose of this area, just as she doesn't divulge the past of her station. But Selena Kriptos knows firsthand why these places were needed. It was at her order that not only this complex but the station itself was built over 50 years ago. She was trying to find a dimension from which the Fustix Fluffy would not be driven out, and this zone was to become a permanent home for their civilization. This would be followed by settlement on the then-pure Del-Alfomar. But this was not destined to happen, for the planet was given over to toxic waste, and Selena handed the station over to Sirena.

###### Sewer.
It became overrun with Slimenoid after someone dumped toxic waste mixed with syrup from Quand Industries into it. Cleaning this area was the main purpose of Siren's request, but the location isn't all that huge, save for the tunnels, which are large enough for an adult Fustix to walk through without bending over and even have a little room to jump. However, it's not exactly spacious enough for three-person battles against Slimenoid.

### Lesoto (AfH)

The planet is a deciduous forest. Wood has an extremely limited range of uses, given the space age status of most intelligent races, but it is still required for certain market applications. It was on this planet that the largest deforestation occurred during the AfH campaign. Although, of course, it doesn't compare to the scale of similar deforestation in the ΛCDM world.

The planet contains one location:

###### Logging Log
An optional quest from Tonata's Rangers, given to Witya immediately upon joining. He arrives at this location to stop Quand Industries, which is actively stealing trees for a makeshift planet. He receives a Woodcutter Axe, or more accurately, a launcher of homing disc blades, from the company that bought the forest plot and whose trees were actually being stolen.

---

### Wealth Refussi (AfH)

This was the planet Quand Industries was assembling for the paid relocation of the Grable from their homeworld. Despite all the efforts of Tonata's Rangers, the planet was successfully assembled. After Witya gutted Acatadop, Quand personally blew up his creation to prevent it from falling into the Tonata government's social fund. The Tonata government was attempting to nationalize it for the purpose of free relocation of all those Quand had expelled from their homeworlds.

The planet contains two locations:

###### Wealth Orbit
Here, Witya engages in space combat with the Quand Industries fleet. Or rather, tries to fly past them without being destroyed, as the protective shields of the Grable Cruisers and the Chairman Lucino Quand flagship are too much for a single small starfighter.

###### Snapped Harmony
A piece of Harmony Quit, embedded in this Frankenstein-like planet, still contains some Resonance Charoite. Witya breaks through here to retrieve one of its fragments as the final component for assembling D.Y.N.O., without which he will be unable to breach the defensive bulkheads of Acatadop Station.
